How to Reach
✈️ By Air
The nearest airport is Pakyong Airport (Gangtok), while Bagdogra Airport (West Bengal) offers better connectivity from major Indian cities.
🚆 By Train
The nearest major railway station is New Jalpaiguri (NJP), approximately 120 km from Gangtok.
🚗 By Road
Sikkim is well connected by road from Siliguri, Darjeeling and Bagdogra via NH-10. Shared taxis, private cabs and buses are easily available.
