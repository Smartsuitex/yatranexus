One-Line Description
Discover Uttar Pradesh, the heart of India's spiritual heritage, where sacred temples, ancient cities, Mughal monuments, wildlife and vibrant culture create unforgettable travel experiences.
Top Experiences
🛕 Kashi Vishwanath Temple
🛕 Shri Ram Janmabhoomi, Ayodhya
🕌 Taj Mahal, Agra
🙏 Triveni Sangam, Prayagraj
🦏 Dudhwa National Park
🌸 Mathura & Vrindavan
How to Reach
✈️ By Air
Major airports include Lucknow, Varanasi, Ayodhya, Agra, Prayagraj, Kanpur and Gorakhpur, with excellent connectivity to major Indian cities.
🚆 By Train
Uttar Pradesh has one of India's largest railway networks. Lucknow, Varanasi, Agra, Prayagraj, Ayodhya and Kanpur are major railway hubs.
🚗 By Road
An extensive network of national expressways and highways connects Uttar Pradesh with Delhi, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Bihar, Uttarakhand and Haryana.
