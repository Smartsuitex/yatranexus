🏔️ Ladakh Tour Packages
One Line Description
Experience the breathtaking landscapes of Ladakh, where majestic mountains, crystal-clear lakes, ancient monasteries, and thrilling road trips create an unforgettable Himalayan adventure.
Top Experiences
🏍️ Khardung La Pass
💙 Pangong Lake
🐪 Nubra Valley Camel Safari
🏯 Hemis & Thiksey Monastery
🏞️ Magnetic Hill
🚗 Srinagar–Leh & Manali–Leh Road Trip
How to Reach
✈️ By AirLeh's Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport is well connected with Delhi, Mumbai and other major Indian cities.
🚆 By TrainThere is no railway station in Ladakh. The nearest major railway stations are Jammu Tawi and Chandigarh.
🚗 By RoadLadakh can be reached via the Srinagar–Leh Highway and the Manali–Leh Highway, both offering spectacular Himalayan road journeys.
