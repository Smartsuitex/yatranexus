How to Reach
✈️ By Air
The fastest way to reach Andaman is via Veer Savarkar International Airport, Port Blair, with regular flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru and Hyderabad.
🚢 By Sea
Passenger ships operate from Chennai, Kolkata and Visakhapatnam to Port Blair, though flights are the preferred option for most travellers.
🚤 Inter-Island Transport
Government and private ferries connect Port Blair, Havelock (Swaraj Dweep), Neil (Shaheed Dweep), Ross Island and North Bay, making island hopping convenient.
