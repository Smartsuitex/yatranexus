🚗 How to Reach Himachal Pradesh
✈️ By Air
Himachal Pradesh is well connected by airports at Shimla, Kullu (Bhuntar), Kangra (Dharamshala) and Chandigarh, with regular flights from major Indian cities.
🚆 By Train
The nearest major railway stations are Kalka, Chandigarh, Pathankot and Una, offering convenient connectivity to different parts of Himachal Pradesh.
🚗 By Road
Himachal Pradesh has an excellent road network connecting Delhi, Chandigarh, Punjab, Haryana and Uttarakhand. Regular Volvo buses, private coaches and taxis are available to major destinations like Shimla, Manali, Dharamshala, Dalhousie and Spiti Valley.
