How to Reach
✈️ By Air: Biju Patnaik International Airport (Bhubaneswar) offers direct flights from major Indian cities.
🚆 By Train: Bhubaneswar, Puri, Cuttack and Berhampur are well connected by Indian Railways.
🚗 By Road: Odisha has excellent highway connectivity with West Bengal, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.
