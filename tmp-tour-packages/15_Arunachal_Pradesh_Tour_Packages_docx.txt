How to Reach
✈️ By Air
The nearest airport is Tezpur Airport, while Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport (Guwahati) offers the best connectivity to Arunachal Pradesh.
🚆 By Train
The nearest major railway station is Guwahati Railway Station. Travelers can continue to Arunachal Pradesh by road.
🚗 By Road
Arunachal Pradesh is well connected by road from Guwahati, Tezpur and Bhalukpong. Private taxis and tourist vehicles are the most convenient options.
