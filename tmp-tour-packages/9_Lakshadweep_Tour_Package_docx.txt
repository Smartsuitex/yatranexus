🏝️ Lakshadweep State Home Page
One-Line Description
Discover Lakshadweep, India's hidden tropical paradise, where turquoise lagoons, coral reefs, untouched islands and serene beaches offer an unforgettable island escape.
Top Experiences
🏝️ Agatti Island
🌊 Bangaram Island
🤿 Scuba Diving & Snorkeling
🚤 Glass Bottom Boat Ride
🌅 Sunset at Kadmat Beach
🐠 Coral Reef Exploration
How to Reach
✈️ By Air
Lakshadweep is primarily accessed via Agatti Airport, with regular flights from Kochi (Cochin).
🚢 By Sea
Passenger ships operated from Kochi connect Agatti and other inhabited islands. Advance booking is recommended.
🚤 Inter-Island Transport
Speed boats and government ferries connect Agatti, Bangaram, Kadmat, Kavaratti and Kalpeni Islands.
