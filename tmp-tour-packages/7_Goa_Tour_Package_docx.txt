How to Reach
✈️ By Air
Goa is served by Manohar International Airport (Mopa) and Dabolim Airport, offering excellent connectivity with major Indian cities and international destinations.
🚆 By Train
Major railway stations include Madgaon (Margao), Vasco da Gama and Thivim, with regular trains from Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru and other cities.
🚗 By Road
Goa is well connected by national highways from Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Mangalore. Luxury buses, taxis and self-drive cars are popular travel options.
