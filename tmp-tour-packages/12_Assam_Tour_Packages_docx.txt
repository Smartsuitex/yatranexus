How to Reach
✈️ By Air
Assam is well connected through Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport (Guwahati), with regular flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Chennai and other major Indian cities.
🚆 By Train
Major railway stations include Guwahati, Dibrugarh, Jorhat, Silchar and Tinsukia, offering excellent connectivity across India.
🚗 By Road
Assam has a well-developed road network connected via NH-27 and NH-17, making travel convenient from neighbouring North Eastern states and West Bengal.
