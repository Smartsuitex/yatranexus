How to Reach
By Air
Nearest airport is Shillong Airport, while Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport (Guwahati) offers the best connectivity from major Indian cities.
By Train
Meghalaya does not have a major railway station. The nearest major railhead is Guwahati Railway Station.
By Road
Excellent road connectivity from Guwahati to Shillong, Cherrapunji, Dawki and other tourist destinations.
