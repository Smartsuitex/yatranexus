How to Reach
✈️ By Air: Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport (Kolkata) and Bagdogra Airport (for North Bengal & Darjeeling) provide excellent connectivity.
🚆 By Train: Kolkata, New Jalpaiguri (NJP), Howrah, Sealdah and Siliguri are well-connected to major Indian cities.
🚗 By Road: West Bengal has an extensive highway network connecting Sikkim, Bihar, Jharkhand, Odisha, Assam and the North-Eastern states.
