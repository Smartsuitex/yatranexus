How to Reach
✈️ By Air:Start your journey from Biju Patnaik International Airport (Bhubaneswar) or Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport (Kolkata). For Darjeeling tours, Bagdogra Airport is the nearest airport.
🚆 By Train:Major railway stations include Bhubaneswar, Puri, Cuttack, Howrah, Sealdah and New Jalpaiguri (NJP), offering excellent connectivity across India.
🚗 By Road:Odisha and West Bengal are well connected via national highways, making road travel convenient between Bhubaneswar, Puri, Kolkata, Darjeeling and Sundarbans with comfortable intercity routes.
