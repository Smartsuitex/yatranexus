How to Reach
✈️ By Air
Major gateways include Guwahati, Bagdogra, Dibrugarh, Shillong and Pakyong Airports, offering excellent connectivity to the North East.
🚆 By Train
Guwahati and New Jalpaiguri (NJP) are the primary railway hubs connecting North East India with the rest of the country.
🚗 By Road
The North East is well connected through national highways from Assam, making road travel convenient between all major destinations.
