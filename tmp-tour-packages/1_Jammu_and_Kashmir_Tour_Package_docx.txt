How to Reach
✈️ By Air
Srinagar International Airport is well connected with major cities like Delhi, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru and Chandigarh.
🚆 By Train
Jammu Tawi Railway Station is the nearest major railhead. From Jammu, Srinagar is approximately 270 km by road.
🚗 By Road
Kashmir is easily accessible via NH-44 from Jammu. Regular taxis, buses and private vehicles operate throughout the year.
Frequently Asked Questions
1. What is the best time to visit Kashmir?
March to June is ideal for sightseeing, while December to February is perfect for snowfall and winter activities.
2. How many days are enough for a Kashmir trip?
A 5 to 7-day trip is ideal to explore Srinagar, Gulmarg, Sonmarg and Pahalgam comfortably.
3. Is Kashmir suitable for family vacations?
Yes, Kashmir is an excellent destination for families, couples, honeymooners and nature lovers.
4. Is airport transfer included in YatraNexus packages?
Yes, airport transfers are included in most packages unless mentioned otherwise.
